N Nivli

Remember when.

Nivli remembers when you last did something — changed the air filter, watered the plants, renewed the passport — and reminds you when it’s time again.

Download on the App StoreFree · iPhone · iOS 18 or later

No account. No tracking. Your things stay on your iPhone — or in your own iCloud when you share them with your household.

A memory for the upkeep of life

Log it once

Type or say what you did — “Changed the air filter.” Nivli files it under the right thing and keeps the whole history.

Ask any time

“When did I last…?” gets a straight answer: 3 months, 2 weeks ago — on June 1. Ask more — “how often?”, “what’s overdue?” — in the app or from Siri.

Reminded when it’s time

One-off or repeating reminders that roll forward when you log. Notifications with Did it, Tomorrow, Next week.

Snap the label

A photo on any entry

The size on the filter box, the receipt, the meter reading. Add it from your photos or take it on the spot — it stays with the entry, on your iPhone.

Search what’s printed

Nivli reads the text in the photo on your phone, so “16x25” finds the day you bought that filter. Free for everyone.

Share it with your household

One home, one memory

Create a household and invite family with Apple’s sharing sheet. Switch a thing to “Share with household” and it shows up on everyone’s iPhone with its whole history.

Anyone can log it

Whoever changed the filter taps Log now, and the history says “Logged by Sam”. Reminders reach every member, and Did it on any phone updates the rest. Works offline, syncs later.

Shared things live in the creator’s own iCloud, seen only by the people they invite. Creating a household is part of Nivli+; joining is free.

Just say it

  • “Hey Siri, log something in Nivli”Siri asks what happened. “I changed the air filter.”
  • “Hey Siri, log air filter in Nivli”Existing things are recognised by name.
  • “Hey Siri, when did I last change the air filter in Nivli?”“3 months, 2 weeks ago — on June 1.”
  • “Hey Siri, remind me in Nivli”Siri asks what and when. “In one year about my passport.”
  • “Hey Siri, what’s coming up in Nivli?”The things due soon.
  • “Hey Siri, remember something in Nivli”“What should I remember?” — “The furnace filter is 16x25x1.” Later: “Ask Nivli” — “What size is the furnace filter?”
  • “Hey Siri, ask Nivli”“What do you want to know?” — “How many times did I water the plants this month?” “What’s overdue?” “What did I do last week?” Type the same into search on Home for an answer card. No AI model, nothing leaves your phone.

Private by design

Nivli has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code. Your things, logs, and reminders live in the app on your iPhone and in your own device backup. Things you share with your household are stored in the creator’s own iCloud account, visible only to the people invited — never to us. Photos you attach are stored in the app, and the text in them is read on the device. The only permissions it asks for are Notifications, if you set a reminder, and the camera, if you tap Take photo. App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.
